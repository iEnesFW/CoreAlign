# Entering and posting a vendor bill

Open the Vendor Bills page from the sidebar and click New bill. Select the vendor, enter the bill number, date and due date; optionally link a Purchase Order and use Load from PO to pull in the lines. For itemized entry, add each line's product, quantity, unit price and VAT %, then click Save to create the bill as a draft.

From the list, process the bill with Post to ledger; the vendor ledger and accounting entries update automatically. If the three-way-match tolerance is breached, the bill drops to Pending Approval instead of posting.

Approve a Pending Approval bill with Approve and post to post it to the ledger. A posted bill can later be paid with Pay, and the GL entry icon opens the resulting journal entry.
