# Receiving goods (goods receipt)

Goods are received against an approved purchase order. Open the Purchase Orders page from the sidebar and, on a row whose status is Approved or Partially Received, click the Receipt action.

In the Goods Receipt dialog, select the Receiving warehouse, enter the Received qty for each line (it cannot exceed the remaining quantity) and optionally fill in the Notes / delivery note # field. Click Post receipt; stock and weighted-average cost are updated and a goods receipt note (GRN) number is generated.

To review the resulting notes, open the Goods Receipts page from the sidebar; expand a row to inspect products, quantities and costs, and use Reverse when a receipt must be backed out.
