# Creating a withholding-tax invoice

The withholding rate is set on the order line; the invoice is then generated from that order. Go to the Orders page from the sidebar and click New order. Choose the customer and add line items.

For each line, alongside product, quantity and unit price, enter the withholding rate in the "WHT%" field (also pick the VAT rate in the line's Tax field). The line total is computed net of withholding. Save the order.

From the order list or the order detail, click Generate invoice and confirm. The generated invoice carries the line withholding amounts; the Withholding total shows as a separate line in the invoice financial breakdown.
