# Recording a payment and applying it to an invoice

Open the Invoices page from the sidebar, open the invoice to collect against, and click Record payment. In the Record payment dialog, enter the payment date, amount and method (Bank Transfer, Cash, Credit Card or Check); add a reference/transaction number if needed.

Under Apply to open invoices, tick the invoices and type the amounts to apply, or use Auto apply (FIFO) to distribute the amount. Confirm with Record payment; the payment is auto-confirmed and the invoice moves to PartiallyPaid or Paid depending on the amount applied. You can also record the same payment from the Ledger tab on the customer record using Record payment.
