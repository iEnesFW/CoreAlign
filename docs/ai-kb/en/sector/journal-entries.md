# Journal Entries

A journal entry is the basis of double-entry accounting: in every transaction total debit must equal total credit. In CoreAlign most journal entries are produced automatically from invoices, payments, stock, and payroll (GL posting); you can also enter them manually.

A Draft entry can be edited; once Posted it affects account balances and becomes immutable (use a reversing entry to correct it). You cannot post into a closed accounting period.
