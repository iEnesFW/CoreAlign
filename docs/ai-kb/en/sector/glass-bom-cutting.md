# Glass BOM and Cutting Optimization

When a glass enclosure design is complete, the system explodes panels and profiles into a bill of materials (BOM). Cutting optimization computes the layout that yields the parts with minimal waste from glass sheets and profile bars (2D nesting and 1D bar cutting).

This lowers material cost and scrap. The BOM and cutting plan are shown on the project detail and passed to production.
