# Glass Enclosure Projects

The Glass Enclosure module lets you model projects such as balustrades, shower cabins, and facades in a 3D designer. Enter dimensions, place panels and hardware, and get automatic pricing (CPQ) and a bill of materials (BOM).

To start, use the New Project wizard on the Projects page; you can attach field survey (measurement) data. An approved design can be converted into an order and a work order.
