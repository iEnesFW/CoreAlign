# Year-end close

Go to the Year-End Close page from the sidebar. Pick the Fiscal Year with the arrow buttons and click Run Year-End Close; once you confirm, the 6xx revenue/expense accounts sweep into 690, and from there into equity account 590 (profit) or 591 (loss) based on the Period Result.

After closing, use Create Opening Entry to carry the balance-sheet balances forward into the next year. While the opening entry has not yet been created you can use Reverse Close to reverse the close; once the opening is consumed, reversal is no longer available.
