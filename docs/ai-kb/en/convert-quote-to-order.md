# Converting a quote to an order

On the Quotes page a quote becomes an order after the customer accepts it. The quote lifecycle is Draft → Sent → Accepted → Order.

1. Open the Quotes page from the left menu.
2. Use the "Send" button on the quote row to send a Draft quote to the customer; the quote moves to "Sent".
3. When the customer accepts, click "Accept" on the quote row (or "Reject" to decline); the status becomes "Accepted".
4. On an accepted quote that has not been converted yet, click "Convert to order".

After conversion CoreAlign creates the new order and takes you to the Orders page. To create a new quote, use the "New quote" button at the top right of the page.
