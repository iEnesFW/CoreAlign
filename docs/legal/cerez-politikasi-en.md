**{{tenantName}}** uses cookies to deliver our services and improve the user experience. This document explains which cookies we use and how you can manage them.

## 1. Cookie Categories

| Category  | Purpose                                  | Retention |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------- |
| Essential | Sign-in, security, language preference   | 12 months |
| Analytics | Page views, error monitoring (anonymous) | 6 months  |
| Marketing | Ad performance, segmentation             | 6 months  |

Essential cookies are **required** and cannot be disabled. Analytics and marketing cookies are loaded only with your **explicit consent**.

## 2. Managing Your Preferences

Use the cookie banner buttons "Accept all", "Essential only" or "Manage preferences". Your choice is stored in your browser for 12 months and will be re-requested after that.

You can clear all cookies from your browser settings; some features of the service may stop working.

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

If the analytics or marketing categories are enabled, third-party services such as Google Analytics may set their own cookies. Consult the privacy policy of each provider for details.
